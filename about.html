About Us

Excellence in Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

Overview

Pushyati® Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted third-party pharma manufacturer with modern infrastructure focused on both nutraceutical and pharmaceutical formulations.

We maintain excellent quality assurance systems from raw material procurement to final packaging, ensuring versatility in dosage forms including tablets, syrups, and nutraceuticals.

Our commitment to quality, scalability, and regulatory compliance helps us maintain consistency and trust in our pharmaceutical and nutraceutical portfolio.

Our Founder

Leadership committed to excellence

Leadership Vision

Our leadership is committed to creating a workplace where every individual feels valued, respected, and motivated to achieve their highest potential. We believe in building a strong organization on a supportive and family-like environment.

To deliver high-quality pharmaceutical and nutraceutical products that improve health and wellness, while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and regulatory compliance.
