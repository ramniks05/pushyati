Our Key Partners

Trusted manufacturing facilities with global certifications

Akums Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Ltd.

Overview: A reputed and large-scale pharmaceutical manufacturer in India, known for advanced infrastructure and stringent quality systems.

Certifications:
WHO-GMP EU-GMP ISO 9001:2015 ISO 14001:2015 GLP NABL (ISO 17025)
Capabilities:
  • Production of large-scale pharmaceutical formulations (tablets, capsules, injectables)
  • Robust R&D and validation labs
  • High-volume capacity for long-term, scalable partnerships

Value to Us: We leverage their high-standard GMP processes and EU certification to manufacture premium, export-quality pharmaceuticals under our brand.

Life Vision Healthcare

Overview: A trusted third-party pharma manufacturer with modern infrastructure focused on both nutraceutical and pharmaceutical formulations.

Certifications:
WHO-GMP ISO 9001
Strengths:
  • Excellent quality assurance systems from raw material procurement to final packaging
  • Versatility in dosage forms (tablets, syrups, nutraceuticals)
  • Flexible capacity for both small- and large-batch manufacturing

Value to Us: Their commitment to quality, scalability, and regulatory compliance helps us maintain consistency and trust in our nutraceutical portfolio.
